# Let's Decide - web app ideas

The starter in this repo (`index.html`) is a spin-the-wheel picker. Open it in a browser to try it.

## Built around the name "Let's Decide"
1. **Group Decider** - share a link; everyone adds options and votes (ranked choice). It shows a live winner. Good for "where should we eat" in a group chat.
2. **Tournament Bracket Picker** - can't choose between 16 things? It pits them head to head, two at a time, until one wins.
3. **This or That** - a swipe-style app: two options, tap the one you like more, and it learns your favourite.
4. **Pros & Cons Scorer** - list pros and cons, give each a weight, get a score and a gut check ("you seem to already want option B").
5. **Restaurant Roulette** - uses your location to suggest a nearby place to eat, with filters for price and distance.
6. **Movie Night Decider** - each person picks a few movies; the app narrows them down round by round.
7. **Decision Journal** - write down big decisions and why you made them, then check back in a month to see how they turned out.

## Other ideas
8. **Chore Wheel** - split chores fairly between roommates or family and rotate them every week.
9. **Pickup Game Team Maker** - type in names (and skill levels if you want) and get two even teams. Pairs well with your marble group maker.
10. **Habit Streaks** - simple daily check-offs with streaks and a calendar heat map.
11. **Trip Splitter** - log who paid for what on a trip and see who owes whom.
12. **Gift List** - keep gift ideas for people in one place, with birthdays and budget.
13. **Study Flashcards** - paste notes and get flashcards with a spaced-repetition quiz.
14. **Workout Randomizer** - pick the time and equipment you have and get a random workout with a timer.
